FAQ

Questions before you decide?

What should I do next in life?

Start with the decision in front of you. Clarify what matters now, narrow the possibilities and identify a practical next step instead of trying to solve your entire future at once.

How do I decide what to do next when I have too many options?

Define what you're actually deciding, identify your priorities and compare possibilities against those priorities. The aim is useful clarity—not perfect certainty.

How can I stop overthinking decisions?

Give the decision structure. Define the actual choice, separate useful information from assumptions, identify what matters and turn the decision into a concrete next action.

Is this a career clarity guide?

It can be useful for career direction, career changes and professional choices, as well as broader life decisions and personal projects.

Who is this guide for?

It's for people who feel stuck, have too many possibilities, are considering a change or simply want more clarity about their next step.
